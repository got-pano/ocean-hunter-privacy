Ocean Hunter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Ocean Hunter is a single-player, offline game. We respect your privacy and designed the game to collect nothing about you.

Information we collect

None. Ocean Hunter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Game progress

Your game progress (levels, coins, upgrades) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Children

Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect no information from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
